Ionic Liquid Conductivity Prediction

Graph Neural Networks Multilayer Perceptron Regression Molecular Features Ionic Liquids

Prediction of properties of ionic liquids using graph neural networks.

Abstract

Predicting relevant ionic liquid (IL) properties such as ionic conductivity using machine learning is challenging because of the large and diverse chemical design space. This study investigates the influence of feature representation on predictive accuracy using a graph neural network and a multilayer perceptron trained on a dataset of 2,684 ionic liquids. Three feature sets are systematically compared: structural features learned through graph neural networks, molecular features, and a combination of structural and molecular features. Structural features alone outperform molecular features alone, highlighting the importance of chemical structure in characterising IL ionic conductivity. The best-performing model combines structural and molecular features, achieving a mean absolute error of 0.470, a root mean squared error of 0.677, and a coefficient of determination (R²) of 0.937. These findings demonstrate the importance of selecting complementary molecular and structural representations for accurate ionic liquid property prediction.
